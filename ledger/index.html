Loans

Enter what you still owe and the monthly payment. Each ticked month takes one monthly payment off the amount in “After payments”. Ticks are a record only: nothing is added to your card bills.

Fixed monthly expenses

Each row is one charge on its bill date and lands in that month’s card bill. Tick “Repeats monthly” for a charge that recurs every month from that date. Statement day is set per card in the credit card table. A bill date after a card’s statement day goes into the next month’s bill (for example, HSBC statement day 5: 6 Oct to 5 Nov is the November statement). Leave it blank for no cut-off. Choose Bank for a charge paid straight from your bank account: it is deducted on its bill date in the balance timeline and is not added to any card bill. The total at the top is the amount still to be billed: expenses dated after your last-updated date, each counted once.
